Notre héritage

A heritage you can taste, read, sing, and walk through.

Whether your family has lived in the same Breton village for generations or arrived in Marseille last decade, whether you grew up in Lyon, Lille, Martinique or Montréal, French heritage is something you belong to and help write.

I.

La langue

Language

The language of the dinner-table debate and the bedtime story. Of regional words your grandparents used that no dictionary quite captures. Of Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Basque, Corsican and Creole, all part of the family. Every phrase you keep is a thread back to someone who spoke it before you.

II.

La table

Food

Sunday lunches that last until evening. Bread from the corner boulangerie, a pot simmering for hours, the dish your family insists only they make properly.

III.

L’art

Art & craft

From cathedral stone to café chansons, from lace and pottery to cinema and comics. Art isn’t only in museums; it’s in the hands that taught you to fold, stitch, paint or play.

IV.

Les lieux

Places

The village square, the market, the coastline, the stairwell of an apartment in the city. The places that shaped your family deserve to be named and remembered.

V.

Les histoires de famille

Family stories

The journeys, the love stories, the hard winters, the funny mishaps retold at every wedding. They live in memory, and memory needs keepers. That can be you.

Cinq gestes simples

Five simple ways to honor your heritage.

No grand gesture required. Pick one this week. Small acts, repeated, are how heritage survives.

  1. 01

    Record their storiesEnregistrez vos aînés

    Sit with a grandparent or elder, press record on your phone, and ask: Where did you grow up? What did Sundays look like? What do you want us to remember?

  2. 02

    Cook the family recipeCuisinez la recette

    Find the dish that tastes like home. Cook it with someone, write the recipe down properly, and note who taught it to whom.

  3. 03

    Learn a phraseApprenez une expression

    A saying, a toast, a word from your region’s language. Teach it to a child. Use it at the table.

  4. 04

    Visit & traceRetracez vos origines

    Visit a family village or town, explore public civil records and archives, or build a simple family tree with the relatives who remember the most.

  5. 05

    Share itPartagez-la

    Post a photo, a recipe or a story with #HonorYourHeritage and invite others to share theirs. Heritage grows when it’s shared.
